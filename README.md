This portfolio brings together three diploma programs I developed and taught at the **Universidad Pedagógica y Tecnológica de Colombia (UPTC)**. The repositories contain course plans, teaching resources, notebooks, and supporting materials across data science, machine learning, and artificial intelligence.

## Programs

| Program | Focus | Course materials |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Diplomado en Ciencia de Datos I** | A broad introduction to data science, from Python and statistics to exploratory analysis and machine learning. | [Open course repository](UPTC-Diplomado-Ciencia-de-Datos-I/README.md) |
| **Diplomado en Ciencia de Datos II** | An advanced path centered on machine learning, its mathematical foundations, data preparation, supervised and unsupervised methods, and deep learning. | [Open course repository](UPTC-Diplomado-Ciencia-de-Datos-II/README.md) |
| **Diplomado en Inteligencia Artificial I** | Artificial intelligence foundations, machine learning, complex networks, deep learning, computer vision, NLP, and time series. | [Open course materials](UPTC-Diplomado-IA-I/) |

## Curriculum highlights

### Diplomado en Ciencia de Datos I

The curriculum introduces data science foundations and computational skills with Python, NumPy, and pandas. It then builds through linear algebra, calculus, probability and statistics, relational databases and SQL, exploratory data analysis, and visualization with Power BI, Matplotlib, and Seaborn. Machine learning topics include supervised methods such as regression and classification, along with unsupervised learning and clustering.

![Data Science Diploma course artwork](UPTC-Diplomado-Ciencia-de-Datos-I/Ciencia%20de%20Datos%20publicidad_2.png)

### Diplomado en Ciencia de Datos II

This program organizes its materials into computational foundations, mathematics, data analytics, supervised methods, unsupervised methods, and deep learning. Topics include Python and Git/GitHub, linear algebra, calculus, descriptive and inferential statistics, preprocessing, dimensionality reduction, and machine learning applications.

### Diplomado en Inteligencia Artificial I

The course repository is organized into eight modules: AI foundations; supervised and unsupervised machine learning; complex networks; deep learning foundations; computer vision; natural language processing; and time series.
